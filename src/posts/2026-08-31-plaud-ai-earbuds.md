Plaud, a company focused on AI note-taking devices, is developing a new pair of earbuds called the Plaud One with eSIM 4G connectivity in the case. The earbuds themselves can be used to record phone calls, and the case can record real-life conversations. The recordings are then sent to their agents to be transcribed and sent back to the user. The first 300 minutes of transcription per month are free, with more time requiring a subscription to an additional plan starting at $8.33 per month.

The case can reportedly last through 25 hours of recording on a single charge and can capture voices within a range of 5 meters, which lines up with the range of most other AI transcription devices. The earbuds have 12mm drivers and include active noise cancellation, making them good earbuds by themselves.

![](https://imgur.com/yqVCsz3.png)

The interesting inclusion of eSIM-powered 4G cellular connectivity allows the device to use Plaud's agents remotely while away from their phone or computer. More agentic features are expected to come soon throughout the next few months through an update.

Plaud already has over 2.5 million people using their products, showing the popularity of the concept of AI note-taking devices.  The device currently retails for $249 and will ship in late 2026.
